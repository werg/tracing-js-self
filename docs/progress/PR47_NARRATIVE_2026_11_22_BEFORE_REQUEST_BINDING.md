### Durable execution and recovery

The bounded V8 state machine now has source-owned nested async calls, generator and Promise scheduling, owned Promise adoption, exact durable commit recovery for coordinator progress/join, causal child bootstrap, and terminal close candidates. The design distinguishes a completed native mutation from a durable producer receipt and from permission to resume or dispatch host work. Recovery checks complete state, request ownership, session/stream/generation and ordinal boundaries, preserved siblings and children, known cumulative costs, clock budget and exact reply semantics. Durable publication/readback and acknowledgement happen before a restored execution becomes runnable. A later durable operation supersedes earlier retry authority. Unknown outcomes remain fenced; they are not treated as known effects and are not redispatched.

**Child creation and child initialization are separate operations.** The causal bootstrap flow links `durable-bootstrap-child` to the exact preceding committed create state and its child description. It validates the complete poststate, child result, operation history and random state. A prior candidate exposed a raw-bootstrap alias: mixed hyphen/underscore spellings could normalize into a mutation after passing a spelling-sensitive guard. The corrected guard normalizes for authorization while preserving original request bytes for journal identity. All four bootstrap spellings refuse before mutation in the corrected native controls. The original red case and all failed attempts are retained; the fix is not retroactively attributed to the old pair.

The separately developed predecessor implementation at native `7f0de371` added unique create-predecessor checks and 11 extra bootstrap assertions. The combined native source preserves the unique predecessor controls, and the completed165/193 hosted recovery gate qualifies its exact composed pair. Subsequent Promise/release composition still needs its own gate. The alias-corrected, predecessor-bound protocol remains the selected integration direction.

Terminal close is also a distinct operation. Native close requires an exact completed/cancelled coordinator state, terminal owned tracks, no pending selection in the adapter and consistent sibling/archive/attempt/settings/random-state boundaries. Known closed state restores as nonresumable and cannot dispatch host work. Producer close history tests lost acknowledgements, altered replies, repeated loss, unknown outcomes, missing intent and close racing an acknowledgement. The prior combined run [45038208676](https://github.com/werg/tracing-js-self/actions/runs/45038208676) completed at 165 native passes and 191/193 producer leaves, with two expectation failures and zero skips. The expectations were corrected to check exact historical attempts and transcript, retained RPC cost plus exactly two recovery/checkpoint RPCs, and zero close requests or host dispatch. The corrected [45042073863](https://github.com/werg/tracing-js-self/actions/runs/45042073863) passed the complete165-native/193-producer gate, including both service-owned positive paths. [Complete successful and failed recovery evidence](docs/evidence/v8-composed-recovery-20261122/README.md) remains retained, alongside the [earlier close evidence](docs/evidence/v8-terminal-hosted-5fc8-2026-11-22/README.md).

Known coordinator commit recovery has separate hosted 62-case proofs for earlier exact pairs. It reconstructs only a proven durable native commit, checks the latest application history and original request/reply, binds owner and generation, preserves cumulative known costs, and charges the retained same-boot monotonic budget through acknowledgement. Those results do not authorize retry of an unknown native outcome or quantify CPU work that was never observed after process loss. The local environment’s clock-namespace limits are not substituted for hosted authority.
